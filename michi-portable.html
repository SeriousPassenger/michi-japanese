Study

Memory

FSRS uses your ratings and the time since your last review. The default memory parameters are fixed; this app does not train personalized parameters.

Initial practice steps: 1 minute and 10 minutes. Forgotten review: 10 minutes. You can return later; the next rating uses the actual elapsed time.

Changing the target affects future ratings. A target is an estimate, not a guarantee when reviews are missed.

Keep your progress

Progress stays in this browser. Export a backup to move between devices or before clearing browser data.

The local files need no connection. Phone installation is available when this folder is served over HTTPS.

How to rate

  • Again: you missed the meaning, or revealed it before recalling.
  • Hard: correct, with real effort.
  • Good: correct, at a comfortable pace.
  • Easy: correct immediately and confidently.

English has many valid translations. Typed answers are compared with reference phrases; you decide whether the meaning is right.

Read the sentence as a whole. Kana helps with reading; fade romaji as you become comfortable.

About the deck

1,000 original sentences aimed at N5-style beginner grammar. These are practice material, not an official JLPT syllabus or a native-speaker-certified course. Some later obligation forms are a small stretch. No vocabulary-only cards.

Combine these sentences with kana study, a basic grammar guide, and listening. Optional audio uses a Japanese voice installed in your browser or operating system; audio availability and offline support vary.

Anki export contains the sentences, readings, translations, and tags; scheduling history is specific to this app.
